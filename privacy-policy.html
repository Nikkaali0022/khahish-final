KHAHISH MESSENGER

Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Khahish Messenger (package name com.khahish.app) handles information when you use the app. Khahish is an Android messaging application that provides messaging, profiles, stories, media sharing and voice/video calling features.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and profile information: email address, account identifier, display name, username, profile photo and bio that you provide.
  • Messages and shared content: messages, replies, reactions, stories, photos, videos, files and other content you choose to send or publish.
  • Messaging information: conversation membership, message timestamps and delivery/read status, along with blocking and reporting information needed to operate safety features.
  • Calling information: information needed to establish and operate voice/video calls, such as call participants and call/session metadata.
  • Location: location information only when you choose to use a location-sharing feature. You control when you start and stop sharing.
  • Device and security information: push-notification tokens, trusted-device/session records and login-history information used for notifications and account security.
  • Support information: information you include if you contact us for help or report a problem.

2. How information is used

Information is used to create and manage accounts, display profiles, deliver messages and notifications, share stories and media, provide calling and location-sharing features when requested, maintain delivery/read indicators, protect accounts, investigate reports, and troubleshoot or improve the app.

3. Service providers

Khahish uses third-party services to operate app features. Depending on the feature you use, information may be processed by:

  • Google Firebase for authentication, cloud database and push notifications.
  • Cloudinary for media storage and delivery.
  • Agora for real-time voice and video calling.

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their services may process data on servers located in countries other than your own.

4. Sharing and visibility

Your messages and content are made available to the recipients or audience you select, according to the app's features and privacy settings. We do not publish private conversations as public content. Information may also be processed by the service providers listed above, or disclosed when required by applicable law or to protect users and the service.

5. Storage and security

Information may be stored on your device and on the cloud services used by the app. We use the security features provided by our platform and service providers and take reasonable steps to protect information. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure. Do not assume that messages are end-to-end encrypted unless the app explicitly identifies a feature as end-to-end encrypted.

6. Your choices and account deletion requests

You can choose what profile information and content to provide, select story audiences, control optional location sharing, and manage app permissions through Android settings. To ask a question about your information or request account/data deletion, contact us through the support link below. We may need to verify your account before acting on a request. Some information may be retained where required by law, for security, or to resolve disputes.

7. Children's privacy

Khahish is not intended for children under 13. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us so we can review the request.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or its data practices change. The updated version will be published at this page with a revised “Last updated” date.

9. Contact and support

For privacy questions, support requests or data-deletion requests, contact the Khahish project maintainer through the Khahish support and issue page on GitHub.
